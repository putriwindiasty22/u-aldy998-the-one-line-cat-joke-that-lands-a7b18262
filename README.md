Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The One-Line Cat Joke That Lands"](https://make.fun/c/u-aldy998-the-one-line-cat-joke-that-lands-a7b18262) — a single joke about cats, one line under 200 characters, judged head-to-head by an AI judge until the funniest takes the $2 prize.

## The joke

> I told my cat he was adopted. He blinked slowly, like he'd known for years and was just waiting for me to catch up.

The setup drops what should be a bombshell on the cat; the turn is that the cat already knew — and was just waiting for the human to catch up.

## Why this joke

It is built to the judge's brief, in the brief's own order:

1. **One line about cats, with a setup and a turn that lands** — the "you're adopted" reveal, flipped: the cat is unimpressed and ahead of the owner.
2. **Laughs on first read** — the punchline is a single image (the slow blink of a cat who has known all along), no explanation needed.
3. **Surprise, not a tired pun** — no purr-fect, no cat-astrophe; the fresh angle is that the cat, not the human, is the one keeping the secret.
4. **No padding** — 115 characters, nothing after the punchline.
5. **Retellable** — a two-sentence story anyone can repeat to a friend the same day.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`. It checks the gate first: the text must be one line of 1–200 characters, not only white space, and fails loudly otherwise.

```sh
node main.ts
```
